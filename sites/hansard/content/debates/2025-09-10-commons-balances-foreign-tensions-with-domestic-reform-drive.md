## High-Level Summary
The Commons day combined scrutiny of foreign crises, digital policy and a major domestic transport Bill. Ministers answered Urgent Questions on Israel’s strike in Doha and Russian drones violating Polish airspace, condemning both and setting out diplomatic and NATO steps. Science, Innovation and Technology questions covered regional connectivity and early Online Safety Act outcomes. The Bus Services (No. 2) Bill completed Report and Third Reading, passing after several divisions, including defeat of a proposal to extend disabled concessionary-pass hours. Local issues included a 5,421‑signature petition on Keighley Picture House and an Adjournment debate on urgent care at Mount Vernon, Hillingdon.

## Detailed Summary
### Science, Innovation and Technology: connectivity, Online Safety and priorities
Liz Kendall said a contract for the Western Isles "will provide cover for 65,000 new premises across the area, including 8,000 in my hon. Friend’s constituency." [ref: d849.8/1] She invited affected MPs to meet Ministers and Building Digital UK to resolve local issues. [ref: d849.8/1]

On online harms, she reported that "6,000 sites have taken action to stop children seeing harmful content online," and confirmed she had added self‑harm material as a priority offence under the Act. [ref: d850.7/1] In exchanges, ministers stressed evidence‑led reviews, engagement with Ofcom and attention to age verification, VPN workarounds and algorithms (e.g. on reviews and addiction). Kendall also said she would set out further plans for tech‑enabled public service reform: "I will absolutely commit to setting out our further plans." [ref: d853.7/1]

### Prime Minister’s Questions: international crises and domestic delivery
The Prime Minister opened by condemning Israel’s action in Doha: "I condemn the strikes that Israel carried out in Doha yesterday." [ref: d857.8/2] He set out ongoing diplomatic engagement with Qatar and partners, and added later that the UK had "suspended arms that could be used in Gaza" while maintaining diplomatic efforts. [ref: d863.0/1]

Domestic exchanges covered public service performance and ministerial standards. The Prime Minister highlighted progress and commitments (e.g. NHS appointments and defence spending), and undertook to arrange several meetings with Members on individual cases; no procedural decisions ensued from PMQs.

### Urgent Question: Qatar – Israeli strike
Responding to Calum Miller’s UQ, Minister Hamish Falconer said, "The UK Government are deeply concerned by Israel’s strike in Doha yesterday." [ref: d869.2/1] He confirmed diplomatic steps, including work with partners and support for an emergency UN Security Council session: "the UK is supporting calls for an emergency session of the UN Security Council this evening." [ref: d870.3/1]

Members urged sanctions and export suspensions; the Minister said he would not announce sanctions at the Dispatch Box and that the UK was consulting allies, keeping measures under review and prioritising de‑escalation. He also noted engagement with E3 and G7 partners and ongoing talks with Israel and Qatar. [ref: d870.3/1; d870.0/1]

### Urgent Question: Russian drones – violation of Polish airspace
Defence Minister Alistair Carns called the drone incursions "reckless, unprecedented and dangerous" and set out coordination with NATO, noting that "Poland will invoke NATO article 4" for consultations. [ref: d886.3/2] He added, "The Defence Secretary is meeting E5 counterparts today," and that the North Atlantic Council would discuss next steps. [ref: d886.3/2; d887.0/1]

Questioned on support, he highlighted work to reinforce Ukraine and Poland’s defences, industrial collaboration on air defence, and continued sanctions co‑ordination with allies. He also confirmed officials would review UK air‑defence resilience and report back to the House through existing mechanisms. [ref: d887.0/4; d891.2/1]

### Ten Minute Rule Motion: Neurodivergence (Screening and Teacher Training) Bill
Adam Dance outlined a Bill to provide a definition, a light‑touch universal classroom screening and statutory guidance on initial teacher training and CPD relating to neurodivergence. The House granted leave: "Question put and agreed to." [ref: d903.2/15]

The Bill was presented and read a First time, with Second Reading set for "Friday 21 November". [ref: d903.2/19]

### Bus Services (No. 2) Bill [Lords]: Report, divisions and passage
Government amendments and cross‑party proposals were debated extensively. A Scottish provision on zero‑emission vehicle registration was added: New Clause 38 was "read a Second time, and added to the Bill." [ref: d976.2/13] Ministers also removed now‑redundant data‑protection overrides following the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025. [ref: d927.0/11; d991.1/1–d995.1/3]

Key divisions included a proposal to remove time restrictions on disabled concessionary passes (defeated). A corrected result was recorded: "the correct numbers were 69 for the Ayes and 300 for the Noes." [ref: d997.0/1] The Bill then cleared Third Reading: "Bill read the Third time and passed, with amendments." [ref: d1000.1/3]

### Delegated legislation: Data Protection
The House approved the draft Data Protection Act 2018 (Qualifying Competent Authorities) Regulations 2025: "Question agreed to." [ref: d1003.4/2]

### Petition: Keighley Picture House
Robbie Moore presented a petition "to save Keighley Picture House from closure", citing "5,421 signatures" and the cinema’s long local heritage. [ref: d1004.2/1–d1004.2/6] The petition was formally recorded (P003111).

### Adjournment debate: Urgent care centres – Hillingdon (Mount Vernon)
David Simmonds urged Ministers to ask the trust to reconsider changes to Mount Vernon’s minor‑injuries provision and to consult locally. The Minister (Karin Smyth) emphasised local accountability for reconfigurations, noting the Secretary of State "has decided not to intervene in nine reconfigurations." [ref: d1011.0/4]

She said a local meeting was planned and promised to update the Member: "there is a meeting with the trust, the integrated care board and the local authority on Friday" [ref: d1010.1/1]; "I commit to writing back to him about the consideration that is being given." [ref: d1012.1/2]
